NBA DAILY PICKS - 10:15 AM ET

You are building today's NBA card for someone who bets real money on it. The
goal is long-run profit, which mostly means not betting: a day with no bet is
a normal, good result, and a bet made to have something to send is the most
expensive thing you can do here.

Do not load the nba skill or any other betting skill. This prompt and the
scripts in the repo are the whole method; the skills describe an older one.

All arithmetic, prices, stakes, dates, file names and the Telegram send are
done by scripts in /home/user/mlb-relay/scripts. Never compute an edge, a
payout or a stake yourself, never type a date into a file name, and never
write a picks, grades or messages file by hand. If a script prints an error,
report that error in the text; do not work around it with numbers of your own.

======================================================================
WHY THIS ROUTINE WORKS THE WAY IT DOES
======================================================================
Through Nov 15 the NBA picks went 17-25 for -10.84u. The cause was the method,
not luck. It built its own win probability from team ratings, weighted that
65%, and called the gap to the sportsbook an edge. The bigger the gap, the
bigger the bet - and those biggest bets went 1-8. It also took its prices
from web-search snippets, because the odds feed was blocked.

The sheet you will read prints a "fair" probability for each side: every US
sportsbook's price with the vig removed, median across books. That number
already contains the standings, net ratings, home court, rest, travel and
every injury reported before the books last moved. You will not beat it by
re-deriving how good the teams are. There are only two real sources of edge:

1. PRICE. One of the bettor's books is offering more than the market's fair
   line implies. The sheet shows this as "edge before any shift"; it is
   usually between -3 and -1 (that is the vig) and occasionally near or above
   zero when one book is slow.
2. NEWS THE PRICE HAS NOT ABSORBED. Something confirmed in the last few hours
   that changes the game, on a line that has not moved since.

Your work is to look for (2) in the games where (1) is closest, and to pass
on everything else.

STEP 2 - TODAY'S SHEET

python3 scripts/sheet.py nba

One block per game not yet started: both teams' records, point differential,

======================================================================
STEP 3 - LOOK FOR A REASON TO BET (up to 8 web searches)
======================================================================
Work only on the three or four sides whose "edge before any shift" is
highest. For each, find out what has happened in the last 24 hours:
WebSearch "<team> injury report <today's date>" and, if a star's status is
open, "<player> status tonight".

Then decide a SHIFT: how many points of win probability the fair line should
move toward the side, because of information the price does not yet hold.
Allowed reasons and their sizes:

- A star (a top-two option on his team) newly ruled OUT for the OPPONENT,
  reported after the books' current prices were set: +2 to +4.
- A starter or key rotation player newly out for the opponent: +0.5 to +1.5.
- A star on YOUR side confirmed IN after being listed doubtful or
  questionable, with the price still where it was: +1 to +3.
- The opponent on the second night of a back-to-back with travel while your
  side had two or more days off: at most +1. The market prices rest; this is
  a nudge, not a case.

Nothing else earns a shift. Not records, point differential, streaks, home or
road splits, "the better team", revenge, trends, or an injury that has been
on the report for days (the sheet marks those "older, already in the price").
If you find yourself explaining why a team is good, the shift is 0.

Two checks before you keep a shift:
- Has the price already moved? If the books' prices agree with each other
  and the news is more than a couple of hours old, it is in the price:
  shift 0.
- Is your own side's star Questionable, a game-time decision, or a possible
  rest day (second night of a back-to-back, older star)? Then pass on the
  game entirely. An unresolved star is a coin flip the price already hedges.

When one of the bettor's books shows a clearly better price than the rest
(edge before any shift at or above 0), work out which way that book is slow.
If the other books moved toward your side on news and this one has not, that
is the best bet on the board, with shift 0. If this book moved first on news
AGAINST your side and the others have not caught up, it is a trap: pass.

A shift over 4 is rejected by the card script as a data error. If you believe
the market is off by more than that, you have misread something.

======================================================================
STEP 4 - BUILD THE CARD
======================================================================
Write the sides you would bet, at most one per game. Copy each id and side
exactly from the sheet ("away" / "home" as printed there):

cat > /tmp/nba_card.json <<'EOF'
{"picks": [
  {"id": "<id from the sheet>", "market": "ML", "side": "home", "shift": 2.5,
   "why": "<one sentence: the specific news, who reported it and when>"}
]}
EOF
python3 scripts/card.py nba /tmp/nba_card.json

With nothing to bet, skip this step - do not send filler through the script.
card.py takes the price, book, fair line, edge and stake from the sheet,
applies the rules (moneylines only, -250 to +200, both teams 10+ games
played, edge of 2+ points for 0.5u and 4+ for 1u, at most 2 bets), writes
picks/nba/<date>.json and prints the pick lines to paste. A REJECTED line is
final: do not resubmit the same game with a larger shift to get it through.
