======================================================================
STEP 5 - ANALYSIS (no web calls)
======================================================================
LAYER 1 - RATINGS MODEL (40%):
  elo_diff  = (SRS_home - SRS_away) * 25 + 100   (home court)
  p_raw     = 1 / (1 + 10^(-elo_diff / 400))
  +1.5 points of probability per point of net-rating gap beyond what SRS shows
  calibrate: p_ml = 0.5 + 0.80 * (p_raw - 0.5)
  injuries: star Out -8 to -12 points of probability, rotation player -2 to -4
  back-to-back: -3 points for the tired team
LAYER 2 - POLYMARKET (weight by liquidity: >$50k 35%, $10k-$50k 28%, <$10k 15%;
  give any shortfall from 35% to layer 1).
LAYER 3 - SPORTSBOOK (25%): no-vig probability from one book's two prices
  p_home = imp(home) / (imp(home) + imp(away)), imp(-150) = 0.600, imp(+130) = 0.435.
BLEND: with Polymarket, weighted as above; without it, 0.65 x ratings + 0.35 x book.

EDGE (vig included - this is the fix):
  price      = the best price for the side among the books in BOOKS
  break_even = |price| / (|price| + 100) for a favorite, 100 / (price + 100) for an underdog
  edge       = (blend - break_even) x 100 points
  A >= 4.0 | B 2.5-3.9 | under 2.5 = pass
SOURCES AGREE = all three layers favor the same team.

SKIP: early-season gate (STEP 1); key injury unresolved; Polymarket and the
book more than 10 points apart with no explanation; no Polymarket AND an edge
under 4.0; a team on a back-to-back and the edge under 4.0; price not real.

STAKES: base 1u (A-tier, sources agree) or 0.5u (A-tier otherwise, or B-tier
