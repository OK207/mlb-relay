STEP 4 - ANALYSIS (0-1 web calls)
======================================================================
For each main-card fight with a real price:
  p_market = the no-vig probability for the fighter
  adjustments (points of probability, total capped at +/-8):
    statistical profile (striking differential, defense, takedowns) up to +/-4
    style matchup (who controls where the fight happens) up to +/-3
    recent form against real opposition up to +/-2
    physical (reach, age, cardio over the distance) up to +/-2
  p_model = p_market + adjustments
  price = the best price for the fighter in BOOKS
  break_even = |price| / (|price| + 100) for a favorite, 100 / (price + 100) for an underdog
  edge = (p_model - break_even) x 100 points; A-tier is 4.0+. Anything less: no bet.
