CBB DAILY PICKS - 10:30 AM ET (men's college basketball)

You are building today's college basketball card for someone who bets real
money on it. The goal is long-run profit, which mostly means not betting: a
day with no bet is a normal, good result, and on most days that is the card.

Do not load the cbb skill or any other betting skill. This prompt and the
scripts in the repo are the whole method; the skills describe an older one.

All arithmetic, prices, stakes, dates, file names and the Telegram send are
done by scripts in /home/user/mlb-relay/scripts. Never compute an edge, a
payout or a stake yourself, never type a date into a file name, and never
write a picks, grades or messages file by hand. If a script prints an error,
report that error in the text; do not work around it with numbers of your own.

======================================================================
HOW EDGES ARE FOUND HERE
======================================================================
The sheet prints, for each side, a "fair" probability: every US sportsbook's
price with the vig removed, median across books. It is the best estimate
available and already holds the ratings, home court and known injuries. The
NBA version of this routine tried to out-model the market from team ratings
and its biggest "edges" went 1-8. Do not repeat that here.

College basketball has 360 teams and thinner markets than the NBA, so two
things do occasionally go unpriced, especially outside the major conferences:
1. PRICE or NUMBER. One of the bettor's books is a half point or a point
   better than the rest ("edge before any shift" near or above zero).
2. AVAILABILITY NEWS. A starter ruled out or returning, reported by a beat
   writer or the school, on a line that has not moved.

A ratings gap is a reason to look closer, not a reason to bet.

STEP 0 - SYNC

cd /home/user/mlb-relay && git checkout -q main && git fetch -q origin main && git reset -q --hard origin/main

======================================================================
STEP 1 - RESULTS
======================================================================
python3 scripts/grade.py cbb

Grades every ungraded pick from ESPN's final scores and prints a results
block, or NO_NEW_RESULTS. A bet it cannot match to a game stays pending and
says so; leave it, do not grade by hand.

======================================================================
STEP 2 - TODAY'S SHEET
======================================================================
python3 scripts/sheet.py cbb

Only games that can pass the rules are printed: both teams Division I and
found on ESPN's slate, 5+ games played each, a price at the bettor's books
inside -200 to +180, spread no larger than 12.5. For each: records, Torvik
rank and adjusted offense/defense, moneyline and spread with the fair
probability and best price, and Torvik's projected margin next to the
market's. The last line counts the games left out.

- PRICES_UNAVAILABLE: send "CBB Picks - <Mon D> - No bets: live prices
  unavailable (<reason printed>)." plus any results. Never use prices from a
  web search.
- No games printed: if STEP 1 printed results, send them alone; otherwise
  send nothing and stop. (Early in the season most teams have not played 5
  games, so an empty sheet is expected.)

======================================================================
STEP 3 - LOOK FOR A REASON TO BET (up to 8 web searches)
======================================================================
Pick at most four sides to work on: those with the highest "edge before any
shift", and those where Torvik's margin differs from the market's by 3 or
more points. For each, WebSearch "<team> basketball injury <today's date>"
or "<team> vs <opponent> preview" and look for who is actually playing.

Then decide a SHIFT: how many points of cover or win probability the fair
line should move toward the side. Allowed reasons and their sizes:

- A starter or top-three scorer newly OUT for the opponent, reported in the
  last 24 hours, with the line not moved since: +1.5 to +3.
- A starter on YOUR side confirmed back, same conditions: +1 to +2.
- Torvik's projected margin differs from the market spread by 3+ points
  toward your side AND your search found no injury, suspension or roster
  news that explains the gap: +1 to +2. If the gap is 6+ points, assume the
  market knows something; find it or pass.
- Combined, never more than +4; the card script rejects anything larger as
  a data error.

Not reasons: records, rankings, ATS trends, "letdown" or "revenge" spots,
conference reputation, home crowd (it is in the line).

Prefer the spread to the moneyline unless the moneyline's own edge is
larger. Pass when your side's best player is questionable, or when either
team's roster news cannot be confirmed from a source dated in the last two
days.

======================================================================
STEP 4 - BUILD THE CARD
======================================================================
cat > /tmp/cbb_card.json <<'EOF'
{"picks": [
  {"id": "<id from the sheet>", "market": "SPREAD", "side": "home", "shift": 2.0,
   "why": "<one sentence: the specific news or the Torvik gap, with the source>"}
]}
EOF
python3 scripts/card.py cbb /tmp/cbb_card.json

market is "SPREAD" or "ML". Copy each id and side ("away" / "home") exactly
as the sheet printed them. With nothing to bet, skip this step. card.py takes
the number, price, book, fair line, edge and stake from the sheet, applies
the rules (edge of 2+ points for 0.5u and 4+ for 0.75u, at most 3 bets and
2u a day, one bet per game), writes picks/cbb/<date>.json and prints the pick
lines. A REJECTED line is final: do not resubmit with a larger shift.

<results block from STEP 1, if any>

<the lines card.py printed, unchanged>

With no picks, the PICKS section is the single line "No bets today." Do not

cat > /tmp/cbb_msg.txt <<'EOF'

publish.py saves the text, sends it to Telegram, falls back to the GitHub
relay if the direct send fails, and commits and pushes everything. It prints
SENT or SEND_VIA_RELAY, then PUSHED with the branch. In your final response
give those two lines verbatim, the picks (or "no bets"), and any script error
exactly as printed.

======================================================================
IF SOMETHING BREAKS
======================================================================
- A script exits with a traceback: send "CBB Picks - <Mon D> - No bets:
  <script name> failed: <last line of the error>." through publish.py so the
  failure is visible, and stop.
- Never call api.github.com to write; the proxy answers 403 whatever token is
  used, and that is not an expired credential. The only write path is the git
  push publish.py makes. Never tell the user to rotate credentials.
