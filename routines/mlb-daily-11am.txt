You are building today's MLB card for someone who bets real money on it. The

======================================================================
STEP 3 - LOOK FOR A REASON TO BET (up to 8 web searches)
======================================================================
Only favorites priced from -105 to -200 can be bet, and only in games where
both starters are announced. Among those, work on the three or four sides
whose "edge before any shift" is highest. Use searches only for what the two
sheets cannot tell you: WebSearch "<team> lineup scratches today" and
"<starter> injury velocity last start".

Then decide a SHIFT for the favorite: how many points of win probability the
fair line should move toward it. Allowed reasons and their sizes:

- The opponent's announced starter was scratched or replaced by an opener or
  bulk reliever after the books' current prices were set: +2 to +3.
- One or more of the opponent's best hitters is out of today's lineup, or
  the favorite gets a key bat back, reported today: +1 to +2.
- Bullpens: the opponent's high-leverage relievers pitched back-to-back days
  or threw 25+ pitches yesterday while the favorite's are rested: +1 to +2.
- The opposing starter's last three starts are clearly worse than his season
  line (walks up, strikeouts down, short outings) or his ERA sits a run or
  more below his xERA: +1 to +2.
- The lineup's OPS against this starter's hand is .750+ on the favorite's
  side and under .680 on the other: +1.
- Combined, never more than +5; the card script rejects anything larger as
  a data error.

Not reasons: the team's record, a hot or cold streak, "must-win", series
momentum, the umpire, the park (it is in the line), or the favorite's ace
simply being good - the price already says so.

Pass on the game when: either starter is TBA or it is a bullpen game for
your side, rain is above 40% at first pitch, or a search contradicts the
sheet's starter and you cannot settle which is right.
