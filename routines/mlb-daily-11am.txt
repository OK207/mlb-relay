======================================================================
STEP 6 - ANALYZE (no web calls)
======================================================================
For each game with confirmed SPs and a real price, weigh:
LAYER 1 - SP MATCHUP (35%): xERA if present, else ERA with K% and BB%;
  handedness against the opposing lineup; last three starts; projected
  length. A bullpen game or opener: flag it.
LAYER 2 - BULLPEN (15%): relief innings over the last 3 days (above ~11 IP is
  taxed); high-leverage arms on back-to-back days or with 25+ pitches
  yesterday are probably unavailable tonight.
LAYER 3 - OFFENSE vs THIS SP (20%): OPS against the SP's hand (>.750 strong,
  <.680 weak), K% against that hand, confirmed absences.
LAYER 4 - PARK + WEATHER (10%): Coors, Great American, Yankee Stadium (LHB)
  inflate; Petco, Oracle suppress. Wind >15 mph out +0.5 runs, in -0.5;
  under 55F -0.2, over 85F +0.2. Rain probability above 40% at first pitch:
  skip the game.
LAYER 5 - UMPIRE (5%): context only; never decides a side.
LAYER 6 - TEAM QUALITY (15%): record and run differential; a team whose run
  differential beats its record is likely better than its record.
LAYER 7 - LINE MOVEMENT: if an earlier bundle today priced the side
  differently, movement of 15+ cents against the pick means downgrade or skip.
LAYER 8 - SCHEDULE (5%): travel, day game after night game, rest.

Then for the side you like:
  p_model    = your win probability for that side
  price      = the best price for that side among the books in BOOKS
  break_even = |price| / (|price| + 100)            (vig included)
  edge       = (p_model - break_even) x 100, in points
  Tiers: A >= 4.0 | B 2.5-3.9 | under 2.5 = pass
SANITY CHECK: compare p_model with the market's no-vig probability (the game
sheet's "no-vig fair", or remove the vig from one book's two prices). A gap
above 8 points is almost always a data error (wrong SP, a scratch, a stale
price), not an edge. Re-check the inputs. If they hold, the pick may still
go, but its edge counts as 4.0 at most.

STAKES (favorites only):
  HIGH CONFIDENCE 2u: A-tier; layers 1-3 agree; SP confirmed; no unresolved
    news; price longer than -200 (at -200 or shorter it is 1u).
  VALUE 1u: B-tier with layer 1 agreeing, or A-tier with one layer against.
  Anything else: pass.
If more than 4 bets qualify, keep the 4 largest edges. Stay within 5u.

TOTALS: only A-tier, only when park, first-pitch weather and the HP umpire all
point the same way, 0.5u, at most one per day.

SKIP when: an SP is unconfirmed or it is a bullpen game; rain above 40%; the
line moved 15+ cents against the pick; the price is not real; the game is not
on the verified slate; any data conflict you cannot resolve.

PRE-PICK CHECKLIST, for every pick:
- Its [G#] is on today's verified slate with status Scheduled or Pre-Game,
  and the team really plays today.
- The SPs match the bundle or statsapi.
- The price is negative, real, fetched today, and the best in BOOKS (name the book).
- The tier, the units and the daily caps follow the rules above.
